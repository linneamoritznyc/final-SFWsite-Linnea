    Learn to produce biologically complete compost that restores soil life, suppresses disease. Questions

Before you ask

Do I need a science background?

No. The courses are designed for people with no relevant experience. And for advanced courses, you'll gain all the knowledge you need here while completing prerequisites which are clearly defined for each path.

I can't afford the courses. Are there options?

We never want insurmountable financial limitations to get in the way of soil regeneration. We are always accepting applications and selections occur on an ongoing basis as we procure funds from donors and other resources. Selection committees place a priority on applicants in under-resourced regions. If you believe you qualify, see the Scholarships page.

What if it's not for me?

Every course carries our 30-day money-back guarantee. Unless a program page states otherwise, you may request a full refund within 30 days of registration provided you have not accessed a substantial portion of the course content. Live in-person events may also have unique limitations. Please see individual program pages for specific details, and the refund policy in our Terms.

Are there any costs above and beyond the listed program enrollment fees?

Sometimes you will, but often you won't. Depending on the program you choose, you may need to procure equipment (ex: For Microscopy you will need access to the right type of microscope, slides, sampling equipment etc.). Each program page provides information about the equipment requirements.

Financial barriers shouldn't keep anyone from caring for the Earth. Scholarships and payment options are growing.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN TO DO

From feedstock to living compost

  1. Choose the browns

    Woody, high-carbon material feeds the fungi.

  2. Add the greens

    Fresh, high-nitrogen material feeds the bacteria.

  3. Watch the heat

    Measure the temperature and turn the pile when it is time.

  4. Check the life

    Look at the finished compost under the microscope before it goes out.
