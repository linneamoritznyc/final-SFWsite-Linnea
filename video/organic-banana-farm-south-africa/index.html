Consultant Case Studies

Organic Banana Farm, South Africa

Shane Plath, 5,000 Acre Organic Banana and more
“Our farm was in serious trouble, but after implementing the SFW approach we increased our yield by 150% in a single season, and we were very successful to repeat this approach on different soils at different farms achieving excellent results. We also cut many input costs such as fertilizers by at least 60%.“ Shane grows banana and several other crops on his 5,000 acre farm in South Africa.
