HOW STEPHANIE USES IT
Each "Jump to" link opens the WordPress page and scrolls straight to the section. Inside that section, "Slot" says which image it is: hero and hero 2 = the two big pictures at the top, left then right; card 1 to 4 = the blog post cards, left to right (the post title is in brackets); image 1 = the video thumbnail next to the text; gallery 1 to 19 = the photo gallery, in the order the photos sit in the page code (on the page they flow into three columns, top to bottom). She uploads the file into that slot, pastes the alt text into the image's Alternative Text field, and the caption into the caption field. Every gallery photo has a caption except the three above.

The post cards (03 to 06) are the posts' own featured images, so they change by themselves when the posts change; they are listed so the folder is complete.

The "Original" line is the full-size source, in case a bigger version is ever needed. You don't need to copy it.
