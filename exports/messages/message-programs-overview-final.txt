HOW STEPHANIE USES IT
Each "Jump to" link opens the WordPress page and scrolls straight to the section. Inside that section, "Slot" says which image it is: hero = the big picture at the top (hero 1, 2, 3 are counted left to right); tile 1, 2, 3... and card 1, 2, 3... are counted left to right, row by row (the title is in brackets); banner = the full-width photo behind the section text; side image or image 1 = the picture next to the text. She uploads the file into that slot, pastes the alt text into the image's Alternative Text field, and the caption (if there is one) into the caption field.

The "Original" line, where there is one, is the full-size source, in case a bigger version is ever needed. You don't need to copy it.
