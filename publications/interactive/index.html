Research

A web of four decades of research

Every paper Dr. Elaine Ingham published touches others: through the organisms it studies, the practices it tests, the systems it was done in, and the colleagues who did the work with her. Follow the threads.

How to read it

Every dot is a paper

Big circles are topics, coloured by kind. Green-ringed circles are the colleagues who wrote three or more papers with Dr. Ingham. A line joins each paper to its topics and its authors, so clusters show where her work gathers. Hover or tap anything to follow its threads; drag to rearrange.
