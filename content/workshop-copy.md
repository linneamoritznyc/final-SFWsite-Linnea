The October 2026 Accelerator Workshop takes place in Tamil Nadu, in southern India. ([India workshop page](https://school.soilfoodweb.com/courses/india-workshop-2026)) Students stay at the [Isha Yoga Center](https://isha.sadhguru.org/), at the foot of the Velliangiri Mountains, about 30 km west of Coimbatore. ([India workshop page](https://school.soilfoodweb.com/courses/india-workshop-2026)) A daily shuttle takes them about 3 km to Sadhguru's Save Soil Regenerative Revolution Farm, where the hands-on work happens. ([India workshop page](https://school.soilfoodweb.com/courses/india-workshop-2026)) The workshop is run together with Isha Outreach, part of the [Isha Foundation](https://isha.sadhguru.org/), and the [Conscious Planet Save Soil](https://consciousplanet.org/) movement. ([India workshop page](https://school.soilfoodweb.com/courses/india-workshop-2026))

Over ten days, students work in small groups, each with an assigned instructor. ([India workshop page](https://school.soilfoodweb.com/courses/india-workshop-2026)) In the first week they build thermophilic BioComplete™ compost piles from scratch: choosing feedstocks, balancing carbon and nitrogen, writing their own recipes, then monitoring, turning and troubleshooting each pile. ([India workshop page](https://school.soilfoodweb.com/courses/india-workshop-2026)) The weekend is free, with optional compost monitoring. ([India workshop page](https://school.soilfoodweb.com/courses/india-workshop-2026)) In the second week they make compost extracts and teas at small and large scale, protozoan infusions and nematode extractions, take soil samples, assess fields, design experiments and apply what they made in the field. ([India workshop page](https://school.soilfoodweb.com/courses/india-workshop-2026)) Microscopy on compost, soil and liquids runs through both weeks. ([India workshop page](https://school.soilfoodweb.com/courses/india-workshop-2026))

The instructors are Loida Vasquez, Director of Advanced Education; Gerald Ramirez, mentor and agronomist; Evan Buckman, Executive Director of the Soil Food Web Foundation; and Kavi Reddy, who leads growth, partnerships and permaculture. ([India workshop page](https://school.soilfoodweb.com/courses/india-workshop-2026)) Students arrive having completed the four Foundation Courses or the two-day Life in the Soils seminar. ([India workshop page](https://school.soilfoodweb.com/courses/india-workshop-2026))

## Why it matters

This is the school's first Accelerator Workshop in India. ([SFW workshops page](https://new.soilfoodweb.com/workshops/)) It feels special. So much of the heart of this organization relates to India, because our mission is so closely linked to food security ([Soil Food Web Foundation](https://new.soilfoodweb.com/?p=321)), and India is where that link is sharpest. India has more undernourished people than any other country: more than 172 million in 2024, about 12 percent of the population. ([Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/amp/story/food/india-has-highest-rate-of-wasting-in-the-world-despite-30-decrease-in-undernourishment-since-2006-un-food-security-nutrition-report)) 18.7 percent of Indian children under five suffer from wasting, the highest rate in the world. ([Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/amp/story/food/india-has-highest-rate-of-wasting-in-the-world-despite-30-decrease-in-undernourishment-since-2006-un-food-security-nutrition-report)) At the same time, 97.85 million hectares of India, 29.7 percent of the country's land, were degraded in 2018 to 2019, up from 28.76 percent in 2003 to 2005. ([Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/news/climate-change/despite-pm-modi-s-assurance-land-degradation-desertification-increasing-78586))

India is also where farming that works with soil biology has grown fastest. In Andhra Pradesh, the state's [Community Managed Natural Farming programme (APCNF)](https://apcnf.in/) (Instagram [@apzbnf](https://www.instagram.com/apzbnf/)) has brought natural farming to 1.8 million farmers through 340,000 women's self-help groups in more than 8,000 villages. ([Food Planet Prize](https://foodplanetprize.org/press/apcnf-wins-the-2026-food-planet-prize/)) In 2026 it won the Food Planet Prize, which describes itself as the world's largest environmental award. ([Food Planet Prize](https://foodplanetprize.org/press/apcnf-wins-the-2026-food-planet-prize/)) The UN Environment Programme reports at least a 30 percent increase in productivity and income for farmers who adopted it. ([UNEP](https://www.unep.org/news-and-stories/story/how-natural-farming-transforming-food-production-southern-india)) A 2026 analysis of the programme's soil science builds on Dr. Elaine Ingham's soil food web research ([Biodiversity for a Livable Climate](https://bio4climate.org/2026/08/12/the-living-soil-a-holistic-analysis-of-nutrient-dynamics-in-andhra-pradesh-community-managed-natural-farming-apcnf/)), and Didi Pershouse, who teaches the school's Soil Sponge Regeneration Workshop, has worked as a trainer and curriculum developer for APCNF. ([Soil Sponge Regeneration Workshop page](https://soilfoodweb.com/soil-sponge-regeneration-workshop/))

Our host partner, the [Conscious Planet Save Soil](https://consciousplanet.org/) movement, was launched by Sadhguru to turn soil loss around through national soil policy. ([Conscious Planet](https://consciousplanet.org/)) In 2022 he rode a motorcycle alone for 100 days and 30,000 km, from London's Trafalgar Square to India, through 27 nations ([Conscious Planet](https://consciousplanet.org/press-releases/sadhguru-kicks-off-100-day-motorcycle-journey-from-london-to-save-soil)), and the campaign reached over 3.9 billion people, with 74 nations offering support. ([Sadhguru blog](https://innerengineering.sadhguru.org/online/blog/riding-to-save-soil-an-epic-journey)) The movement asks governments to require a minimum of 3 to 6 percent organic content in agricultural soils, and Gujarat became the first Indian state to sign a Save Soil agreement. ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/india/gujarat-becomes-first-indian-state-to-sign-mou-with-isha-outreach-to-save-soil)) Its case rests on a United Nations warning that over 90 percent of the earth's soil could become degraded by 2050. ([Conscious Planet](https://consciousplanet.org/press-releases/sadhguru-kicks-off-100-day-motorcycle-journey-from-london-to-save-soil)) The Accelerator Workshop follows the school's two-day Life in the Soils seminar, held at the same Save Soil Farm on 16 and 17 October with the Isha Foundation and Conscious Planet. ([Life in the Soils seminar page](https://school.soilfoodweb.com/courses/life-in-the-soils-seminar-2-day)) Together they give farmers and consultants in India the hands-on skills to build soil organic content back. <mark>[FIND: public link for the free Voices of Regeneration panel in Hyderabad on 10 October, to mention all three India events.]</mark> <mark>[FIND: how many students are from India, and from which states.]</mark>

## Why it matters

Wild Ken Hill is one of England's best-known places where rewilding and food production share the same land. ([Rewilding Britain](https://rewildingbritain.org.uk/rewilding-projects/wild-ken-hill); [Wild Ken Hill](https://wildkenhill.co.uk/about/)) Since 2019, about 1,000 acres of poor-quality farmland on the estate have been rewilded, grazed at low density by Red Poll cattle, Tamworth pigs and Exmoor ponies. ([Rewilding Britain](https://rewildingbritain.org.uk/rewilding-projects/wild-ken-hill)) Within three years, the average number of plant species in survey plots roughly doubled, from 16.8 to 33.2, and rare plants such as smooth cat's-ear spread. ([BirdGuides](https://www.birdguides.com/news/plant-numbers-double-at-wild-ken-hill/)) Two pairs of beavers released in 2020 produced Norfolk's first beaver kit in about 600 years in 2021. ([Forth 1](https://www.hellorayo.co.uk/forth/uk/news/baby-beaver-first-600-years)) Students built soil biology on a working farm surrounded by recovering wild land. <mark>[FIND: whether Wild Ken Hill measures soil biology or soil carbon on its regenerative fields, and whether it would share results for this page.]</mark>

## Why it matters

This was the school's dryland workshop. The country south of Santa Fe lies where four ecoregions meet: the Southern Rocky Mountains, the Arizona/New Mexico Mountains, the Southwestern Tablelands and the Arizona/New Mexico Plateau. ([Santa Fe Conservation Trust](https://sfct.org/protected-lands/galisteo-basin/)) The region has lived through repeated droughts, including 2000 to 2004 and 2021. ([Conservation Biology Institute](https://consbio.org/the-north-america-forest-initiative/southern-rocky-mountain-ecoregion-santa-fe-subregion/)) Synergia Ranch left most of its land to recover from early overgrazing and uses drip irrigation to save water. ([Synergia Ranch](https://synergiaranch.com/agroecology/)) In 2019 New Mexico passed its Healthy Soil Act, which funds farmers and ranchers to raise soil organic matter, microbial activity and water retention, and measures soil microbiology as part of its baseline. ([SiX Agriculture](https://ag.stateinnovation.org/new-mexicos-2019-health-soil-bill-sets-precedent-for-agriculture-policy-as-climate-policy/)) Students here built soil biology in the conditions the state is now investing in. <mark>[FIND: confirm that Synergia Ranch is inside the Galisteo Basin watershed.]</mark>

## Why it matters

Costa Rica shows what a country can do when it decides to restore land. Forest cover fell to 21 to 25 percent of the country in the 1980s, and after policies such as payments to landowners who protect forests, it had climbed back to 53 percent by 2016. ([Inter Press Service](https://www.ipsnews.net/?p=146239)) Farmland has not had the same turnaround: FAO data put Costa Rica first in the world for pesticide use per hectare over 2000 to 2020, at about 23 kg per hectare. ([Semanario Universidad](https://semanariouniversidad.com/pais/costa-rica-es-el-pais-que-utiliza-mas-plaguicidas-en-todo-el-mundo-segun-estadisticas-de-la-fao/)) This workshop taught consultants to replace chemical inputs with soil biology, on a campus where schoolchildren already learn on an organic farm. ([October 2025 newsletter](https://soilfoodweb.com/october-2025-newsletter/)) <mark>[FIND: whether any Costa Rican graduates now consult for local farms, and what they grow.]</mark>
