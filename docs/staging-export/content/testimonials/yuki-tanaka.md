# Yuki Tanaka
Japan
While the educational material itself is top-tier, the global community alone is worth the entire price of admission. I have connected with brilliant peers, found dedicated accountability partners, and even established two international project collaborations. It feels like an exclusive mastermind group where everyone is fully invested in pulling each other up to the next level.
