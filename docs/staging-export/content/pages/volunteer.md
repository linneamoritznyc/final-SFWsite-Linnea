# In service to the soil that sustains us all
The Soil Food Web Foundation is a nonprofit organization carrying forward the life’s work of Dr. Elaine Ingham — empowering communities everywhere to restore and protect the Earth’s soil and living systems.

To honor and grow Dr. Elaine Ingham’s legacy of empowering communities to restore and protect the Earth’s soil and living systems — by sharing teaching and learning about the soil food web, pursuing knowledge related to soil, plant, and planetary health, incubating regenerative agricultural and ecosystem practices, and building community and financial support for soil food web teaching, research, and practice.
Healthy, living soil in every terrestrial ecoregion on the planet — nurtured by local communities who understand why soil health is the foundation of ecosystem, agricultural, planetary, and human wellbeing.
## Rooted in legacy, growing in impact
## Four ways we grow this work
World-class education in soil biology, from foundation courses to professional certification, with workshops in a growing list of ecoregions and support that makes learning accessible.
Replicating key experiments, opening our trial and case-study data, and advancing microscopy and lab methods — so more people understand why the soil food web works.
Ecoregion hubs that integrate education, composting, microscopy, and consulting; partnerships with farmers to trial and document soil food web practices where they live.
A global network of students, volunteers, donors, and advisors who make this mission durable — and welcoming to newcomers.

# The woman who started it all
Dr. Elaine Ingham pioneered research in soil biology across the final four decades of her life and made her science radically accessible — so that anyone, anywhere, could learn to restore the soil beneath their feet.
This Foundation is her living legacy, and we hold it with care: not by asking the world to take her word for it, but by opening her science for the world to prove.
