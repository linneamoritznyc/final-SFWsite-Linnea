- 1981 Ph.D., Colorado State University Elaine Ingham earns her Ph.D. in soil microbiology at Colorado State University, following a B.A., cum laude, from St. Olaf College (1974) and an M.S. in microbiology from Texas A&M University. Her first scientific papers appear in 1982, beginning with work on ultraviolet-induced autofluorescence of mycorrhizal root infections.
- 1985 The foundational paper She co-authors “Interactions of Bacteria, Fungi, and their Nematode Grazers: Effects on Nutrient Cycling and Plant Growth” (Ecological Monographs 55:119–140), detailing the relationships between soil food web trophic groups and their role in plant nutrition.
- 1985–2002 Research and teaching Postdoctoral research at the University of Georgia’s Institute of Ecology, then the faculty of Oregon State University (1986–2002) as Instructor and Associate Professor. She serves as the first President of the Soil Ecology Society, as Program Chair of the Ecological Society of America, and co-authors the USDA’s Soil Biology Primer (1999). Across her career: more than seventy scientific papers, six book chapters, and seventeen technical reports.
- 1996 Soil Foodweb Inc. She founds Soil Foodweb Inc., bringing microscopy-based soil assessment to growers through soil-testing laboratories — later expanding through the Soil Foodweb Institute in Australia.
- 2010–2013 Teaching and research leadership She teaches at Maharishi University of Management (Maharishi International University) (2010–2013) and serves as Chief Scientist at the Rodale Institute (2011).
- 2014 Environmental Celebration Institute She establishes the Environmental Celebration Institute, a farm and research facility in Northern California where she directs research and teaches in person.
- 2019 The Soil Food Web School She creates the Soil Food Web School, bringing her curriculum online. Enrollment grows to more than 10,000 students across 100+ countries.
- 2025 The Soil Food Web Foundation As Dr. Ingham retires from teaching, the Foundation is established to continue her work — holding the school’s programs and intellectual property, with a broader mission of open research, applied practice, and community support.
- 2026 Carrying the work forward Dr. Ingham passed away on February 16, 2026. The Foundation continues the work, dedicated to implementing broadly what Elaine proved and demonstrated throughout her lifetime.
