# Put your hands in this work
The Soil Food Web Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit — gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Every gift grows the work directly: open research the whole world can use, scholarships for students who need them, and ecoregion hubs that turn knowledge into restored land.
## Monthly giving is the steadiest soil we can grow in
Sustaining donors let us plan research seasons, promise scholarships, and build hubs — not just hope for them.
