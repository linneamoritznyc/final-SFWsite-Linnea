# 4,000 acres Norfolk, England
Nick Padwick – 4,000 acres Norfolk, England AT Wild Ken Farm, nick and his team have implemented different regenerative practices such as mixed species cover cropping, minimum tillage, integration of livestock, companion cropping, micro-cropping, strip tillage, margin, headland and hedgerow management, as well as numerous others. Nick has approx. 1.5 miles (—2km) of compost windrows under production at any time.
