# Compost Intensive Course | 2027 Cohort #1
![](https://new.soilfoodweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/CIC-4.png)
## The Time Is Now… And Here’s Why
When made correctly, compost becomes a living biological resource that supports soil health and can improve plant resilience to pests and environmental stressors, increase yields and nutrient content, and enhance human health, all while supporting regenerative growing systems that transform and utilize organic waste products.
Mastering the art of BioComplete™ Compost production takes dedication, practice, and, often, the guidance of a trained professional. This composting process is the cornerstone of the Soil Food Web Approach, which is being used around the world to regenerate degraded soils quickly and effectively
The Soil Food Web School’s Compost Intensive Course aims to take anyone, regardless of educational background, skill level, or experience, and help them produce their own BioComplete™ Compost within 10 weeks.
## About the Course
The Compost Intensive Course is a hands-on, 10-week online training designed to guide you through the process from beginning to end alongside a global cohort of peers.
There are NO prerequisites!
And it counts toward your consultant pathway*
## Why It’s Worth Your Time
More than 80% of the people who took our pilot version of this course walked away with successful thermophilic piles that achieved BioComplete™ biological potency.
We have designed this for maximum learning, with minimum time commitment, while optimizing for long-term impact.
