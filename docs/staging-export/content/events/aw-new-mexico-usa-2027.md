# Accelerator Workshop | New Mexico USA
![](https://new.soilfoodweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_3426-1-scaled.jpg)
A 10-day in-person Accelerator workshop located at Synergia Ranch Retreat Center
( https://synergiaranch.com/about-us/ )  
  links: [https://synergiaranch.com/about-us/](https://synergiaranch.com/about-us/)
This experience is designed to accelerate student learning through BioComplete Compost Production and Liquid Amendments and help you master the art of inoculate grade biological soil amendments in a fast-paced and collaborative environment.
Attendees will work in small groups guided by a mentor, to build, monitor, and maintain compost piles, brew a variety of liquid amendments, and practice microscope analyses with guidance. Microscopy sessions will be provided based on level of experience for beginner and advanced practitioners.
If you are interested in this event, submit this form and select the New Mexico Location: https://school.soilfoodweb.com/pages/workshop-interest  
  links: [https://school.soilfoodweb.com/pages/workshop-interest](https://school.soilfoodweb.com/pages/workshop-interest)
