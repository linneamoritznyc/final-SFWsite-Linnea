# Elena Kalli
School Administration Officer
Elena Kalli brings over 20 years of experience in business operations, strategic planning, and organizational development. Specializing in company culture and HR, she is dedicated to fostering purpose-driven workplaces and optimizing team dynamics. Her expertise lies in refining processes, solving complex challenges, and guiding organizations through critical transitions.Inspired by the transformative work of Soil Food Web, Elena is deeply committed to supporting mentors and students as they work toward their goals. She focuses on creating systems and processes that empower the community, ensuring they have the structure and guidance needed to succeed.Her experience spans multiple industries, including education, consulting, and corporate governance, where she has played a key role in streamlining operations and enhancing efficiency. At Soil Food Web, she helps drive meaningful improvements and supports the team in making a lasting impact.When she’s not focused on helping people grow, Elena enjoys staying active, spending time with her children, and attempting to win the affection of her cats.
