# Casey Williams
SFW Consultant & Mentor
Casey is an agriculture expert with extensive experience, both in theory and practical application, in the areas of permaculture, soil health, and regenerative farming. Over the years, Casey has worked in many capacities in agriculture, including managing farms, doing permaculture design, cultivating gourmet mushrooms and microgreens, building and running aquaponics systems, and producing biochar and compost. Being a certified consultant through the Soil Food Web School and running his business has allowed him to continue to promote regenerative land management through direct application, education, and hands-on mentorship. By constantly experimenting with different composting methods and biochar production methods at various scales, as well as learning diverse approaches to land stewardship, Casey continuously builds on his knowledge base in this complex field.
