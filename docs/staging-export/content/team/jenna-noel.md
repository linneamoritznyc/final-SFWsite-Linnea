# Jenna Noel
Executive Committee
Jenna Noel is the daughter of two pioneering microbiologists, Dr. Elaine Ingham and Dr. Russel Ingham. She has been steeped in soil science her whole life, having spent much of her summers as a child counting nematodes under the microscope and learning to make really good compost. In addition to a successful career in management, Jenna is a proud mother, sports enthusiast, and committed to the Soil Food Web Mission. She represents the Ingham family as an active steward of Dr. Elaine’s living legacy and the ongoing work to promote ecologically healthy food systems around the world.
