# Gerald Ramírez
SFW Mentor
Gerald Ramírez is an Agronomist who graduated from the University of Costa Rica and recently completed his licentiate thesis. Gerald serves as an instructor for CLP and CTP at the school, as well as contributing as a soil analyst in the interpretation of physical and chemical soil indicators for optimizing management practices, with a focus on soil health and sustainability. Additionally, he provides valuable insights into crop management.
Gerald initially joined the Soil Food Web School as a Content Developer but was later promoted to an Instructor role after completing Tier 3 Professional Development with the School. He still actively contributes to content creation, with his areas of expertise being applied mathematics, research, and transitional agriculture.
