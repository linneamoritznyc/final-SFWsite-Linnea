# Stephanie McDaniel
Director of Operations
An avid gardener and a student of the Soil Food Web School, she brings her more than 20 years of experience as a business owner and operator across food systems, online education, fintech, and community-based initiatives to the mission of soil regeneration. Her career focus is to help mission-driven organizations communicate clearly and operate better so they can grow impact without losing their values.
At the Soil Food Web Foundation, her work emphasizes community building, growth outreach, and long-term program sustainability in support of the Foundation’s mission of empowering individuals and organizations to regenerate soil in their communities.
