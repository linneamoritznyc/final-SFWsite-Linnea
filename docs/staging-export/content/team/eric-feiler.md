# Eric Feiler
Executive Committee
Eric graduated cum laude with a Bachelor’s Degree in Anthropology and Archeology from Colorado College. He later earned his Master of Science in Quaternary Studies with an emphasis in Paleoecology and Archeology, graduating with distinction. He served as Co-Founder and Board Member of the PaleoCultural Research Group before becoming the Property Manager for a 200-acre ecological restoration site in Boulder, Utah. Eric also owns High Desert Soil Works, LLC, where he does consulting and specialized compost production.
