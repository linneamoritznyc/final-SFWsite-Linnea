### 🌱 Join the Free Webinar Series: A Living Legacy — The Science of the Soil Food Web
What if the key to restoring our planet — and growing healthier, more resilient food — was hidden beneath our feet?
For over 40 years, Dr Elaine Ingham has shown the world that soil isn’t just “dirt.” It’s a living, breathing ecosystem filled with billions of microorganisms — each playing a vital role in plant health, fertility, and regeneration.
Her groundbreaking work revealed that the life in the soil drives the life above it, changing how thousands of farmers, growers, and scientists manage their land across the globe.
Now, you’re invited to learn directly from the community continuing her legacy.
[Register Now](https://community.soilfoodweb.com/nov2025_webinar/)
### 🎓 What Is A Living Legacy?
It’s a free webinar series hosted by the Soil Food Web Community, celebrating Dr Elaine Ingham’s pioneering research and the growing global movement of soil regeneration.
Across multiple sessions, you’ll discover how soil biology connects to everything — from crop health and nutrient density to climate resilience and ecosystem recovery.
In this inspiring series, you’ll learn: ✅ The original discoveries that revolutionized soil science ✅ How microbes form symbiotic relationships with plants ✅ The practical steps to restore fertility and structure naturally ✅ Real-world success stories from regenerative farmers and consultants
Whether you’re a grower, gardener, scientist, or simply passionate about nature — this series will transform the way you see soil forever.
### 🌍 Why This Matters
Healthy soil isn’t just the foundation of agriculture — it’s the foundation of life.
By understanding and nurturing the Soil Food Web, we can reduce chemical inputs, increase yields, and regenerate landscapes that have been degraded for decades.
It’s not just about sustainability — it’s about regeneration.
### 🌾 Join the Global Movement
This is your invitation to be part of a growing community of people working to restore soil health, protect ecosystems, and ensure a fertile future for generations to come.
