## Linnea's image rules for the website

- Colorful, vibrant, professional photos only.  
- HEIC files are usually iPhone photos: check them closely, since most look grey and flat on the site.  
- Each photo appears in one slot only on the whole site.  
- Avoid group shots where people are posed very close or touching: it can read as cult-like.  
- Only suggest photos Linnea can use: no directory members' own photos, no blog post images, nothing already in a slot elsewhere on the site (unless she approves a repeat).  
- Volunteer page look: candid action, many people working together, hands in the soil, tools in use.
