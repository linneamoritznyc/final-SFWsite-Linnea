Founder

EI

Dr. Elaine Ingham

Founder, 1952 to 2026

Dr. Elaine Ingham was the Soil Food Web School’s founder, principal ambassador, and science and research advisor. She began her college career at St. Olaf College, where she graduated cum laude with a Bachelor of Arts. She earned her Master of Science in Microbiology from Texas A&M and her Ph.D. in Soil Microbiology from Colorado State in 1981. From there she completed her post-doc at the University of Georgia Institute of Ecology, then served as an Instructor and later an Associate Professor for many years at Oregon State University. She served as the first President of the Soil Ecology Society, the Program Director for the Ecological Society of America, and a member of the American Society of Microbiology. She founded Soil Foodweb Inc. in 1996, owned and then sold the Soil Foodweb Institute in Australia, and served as Chief Scientist at the Rodale Institute. Her research was global. Her scientific papers date as far back as 1982, beginning with work on ultraviolet-induced autofluorescence of arbuscular mycorrhizal root infections, and her later work included a comparison of milk from organic and conventional farms in the United States. She wrote 6 book chapters, took part in research teams publishing 17 technical reports, and spoke at agricultural conferences in Great Britain, Brazil, Australia and across the United States. In 2019 she worked with Luke Louka to create the Soil Food Web School.
