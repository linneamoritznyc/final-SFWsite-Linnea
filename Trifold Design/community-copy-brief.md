# Brief: rewrite the community panel of the Soil Food Web Foundation trifold

Paste this whole file into a chat. It is self-contained.

---

I need you to rewrite about 350 characters of body copy for the back panel of a
printed trifold brochure. Read all of it before you write anything.

## What the piece is

A six-panel letter tri-fold for the Soil Food Web Foundation, a 501(c)(3)
nonprofit in Portland, Oregon. It teaches people to see, measure and restore
the biology in soil. Its founder, the soil biologist Dr. Elaine Ingham, died in
February 2026; the Foundation was created in 2025 to carry the work on.

The six panels are: cover, Our story (Dr. Ingham), Teaching, Research,
Practice, and Community. Community is the last panel a reader reaches. It is
the closing argument.

## The panel as it stands

Heading, already fixed and not yours to change:

> **A global community makes this work outlast any one of us.**

Then a photograph of a workshop group around a finished compost pile. Then the
two paragraphs I want replaced:

> Dr. Elaine gave this movement its science. The community gives it staying
> power. Soil Regenerators in more than 100 countries, on six continents, teach
> their neighbors, run their own trials, and restore land farm by farm. Donors,
> volunteers and partners are building something no single organization could
> build alone.
>
> Every one of those roles is open.

Then a row of four icons labelled STUDY, VOLUNTEER, DONATE, HOST A WORKSHOP.
Then a QR code to the free community at school.soilfoodweb.com, captioned
"Recordings, discussion and questions answered, open to anyone, at no cost."
Then the legal block and the tagline "Healing soil. Feeding humanity.
Restoring the living world."

## Why I think it is weak

Tell me if you disagree, but this is my read:

1. "Dr. Elaine gave this movement its science. The community gives it staying
   power" is a tidy antithesis that asserts rather than shows.
2. "teach their neighbors, run their own trials, and restore land farm by farm"
   are generic activity verbs. Any membership organisation could print them.
3. "building something no single organization could build alone" is a cliché
   and is doing no work.
4. "Every one of those roles is open" is flat, and the four icons under it
   already say exactly that.
5. The heading makes a real promise, that the work outlasts its founder, which
   is a live question for a nonprofit whose founder died eighteen months ago.
   The body never answers it. That is the gap.

## Hard rules

These are not style preferences. Copy that breaks one is unusable.

- **Invent nothing.** No number, name, date, place or claim that is not in the
  facts below. If you want a fact that is not there, say so in a note at the
  end instead of writing it.
- No percentages or statistics without a named source.
- No em dashes.
- No the phrase "nature's operating system". No "certified". No "world's
  foremost". No superlatives that nobody has attributed.
- No acronyms in public copy. Write "Foundation Courses", never "FC".
- American spelling. Sentence case in headings.
- Plain declarative sentences. This organisation's voice is a scientist's:
  specific, unhurried, willing to say what is not yet known.
- The reader is a grower, a student, or a potential donor holding a piece of
  paper. Not a board member.

## The only facts you may use

- Students and practitioners in more than 100 countries, on six continents.
  Source: the Foundation's enrollment records, confirmed August 2026.
- Over 10,000 individuals have enrolled in its programs. Same source.
- Graduates hold two titles, earned by assessed work: Soil Food Web Consultant
  (completed all four Advanced Programs, together called the Complete
  Practicum) and Soil Food Web Lab Technician (completed Soil Microscopy).
  Neither title is externally accredited. Lab technicians outnumber consultants
  roughly two to one.
- Members of the community are called Soil Regenerators.
- Eleven growers have filmed their own work and published it, ranging from a
  quarter acre in California to 10,000 acres of corn and soy in Illinois, and
  including a public park on Governors Island, New York.
- The free community carries recordings, discussion and answered questions, and
  is open to anyone at no cost.
- There is a public directory of trained professionals, which practitioners
  maintain themselves.
- The volunteer network is described in the Foundation's own copy as "just
  taking root", so it is small and new. Do not imply otherwise.
- Ecoregion hubs, bringing compost production, microscopy, consulting and
  education under one roof, are in planning and not yet named.
- Dr. Ingham made her science deliberately accessible so that anyone anywhere
  could learn to restore the soil beneath their feet.

## What to give me

Two paragraphs, 350 characters in total, give or take 40. The second may be a

Give me **three distinct versions**, not three rewordings of one idea. Make

Under each, one sentence on what it is doing and what it gives up. Then tell me
