Teaching

Anyone, anywhere, can learn to restore living soil.

From first curiosity to professional practice. Everyone starts with the Foundation Courses. Where you go next depends on whether you want to grow better yourself, or help others do it.

Foundation Courses

The science of the soil food web, the organisms and their roles, and how biological amendments are made and applied. Online, self-paced, about 150 hours, and required before any Advanced Program.

Advanced Programs

Soil Microscopy, BioComplete™ Compost Production, Biological Liquid Amendments and Field Trial, each with its own mentor. All four together are the full path.

Accelerator Workshops

The Advanced Programs worked through as a group on working land, the whole method applied at once. Held periodically, and hosted with a partner on their land. Dates and places go up on the school site.

Where it leads

All four Advanced Programs together carry the Soil Food Web Consultant title; Soil Microscopy alone, the Lab Technician title. Every program, with its price, is listed at school.soilfoodweb.com

The soil beneath our farms and forests is alive.

A web of bacteria, fungi, protozoa, nematodes and microarthropods feeds plants, builds soil, stores carbon and cleans water when it is intact.

For forty-five years, Dr. Elaine Ingham mapped the Soil Food Web and taught the world to restore it. We carry her legacy forward: showing what is possible, teaching how to partner with life in the soil, researching why things work or do not, and getting dirty through direct action on working land. Living soil is the solution, and it is beneath your feet.
