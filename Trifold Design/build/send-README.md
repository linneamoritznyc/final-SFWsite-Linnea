# Send these two files to the print shop

**`SFW-brochure-2026-print-ready.pdf`** — the artwork. Two pages: page 1 is the
outside of the sheet, page 2 is the inside. CMYK, PDF/X-4, bleed and trim marked.

**`PRINT-SPEC.md`** — one page of specifications. Paste it into the email.

## The two previews are not for printing

`preview-page-1-outside.png` and `preview-page-2-inside.png` are the same two
pages as pictures, at 300 DPI, for looking at and sharing. **Do not send them to
the printer.** A PNG carries no trim box, no bleed box, no CMYK separations and
no vector type; a shop handed one would either come back with questions or print
something worse than the PDF.

---

If the shop says their workflow needs PDF/X-1a, or wants the two sheets as
separate files, those versions are in `Trifold Design/print/alternates/`. Send
the PDF above unless they ask.
