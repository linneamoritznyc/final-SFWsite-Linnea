Accelerator Workshop January 2027

Costa Rica · 18–29 January 2027

Photo by Christina Victoria Craft on Unsplash

Overview

Join the Soil Food Web School team in Costa Rica for a ten-day, hands-on Accelerator Workshop. Students build BioComplete™ compost piles from scratch, make extracts and teas, and work at the microscope in small groups with a mentor.
