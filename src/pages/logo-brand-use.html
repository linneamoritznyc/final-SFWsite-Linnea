Brand

Logo and name use

Use of the Soil Food Web Foundation name and mark is by request.

Building something aligned with our mission? Please contact us before using our name or logo. Write to info@soilfoodweb.com with "Logo use" in the subject line, saying where and how you intend to use it.

Board decision: whether logo use stays inquiry-only or becomes a download with usage rules. Evan.
