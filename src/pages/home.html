Soil Food Web Foundation

Join a global community of Soil Regenerators

Healing soil. Feeding humanity. Restoring the living world.

The Soil Food Web Foundation is a nonprofit that carries forward Dr. Elaine Ingham's life's work to support farmers, land stewards and soil enthusiasts by teaching the fundamentals of the Soil Food Web Approach to land management. Our approach includes accessible methods of assessing soil life and growing and applying beneficial soil biology to restore living soil in every bioregion. Through our online and in-person programs, we educate and support the global community of soil regenerators.

Who we are

A foundation built on living soil

For over four decades, Dr. Elaine Ingham showed the world what farmers and foresters have always sensed: soil is not dirt. It is a living web of bacteria, fungi, protozoa, nematodes, and microarthropods that feeds plants, stores carbon, holds water, and sustains every terrestrial ecosystem on Earth.

The Soil Food Web Foundation was created to carry that work forward. We teach the science through the Soil Food Web School, pursue and share new knowledge, incubate regenerative practice in ecoregions around the world, and build the community that makes it all last.

The Soil Food Web Approach

Partner with nature's intelligence

  • {{photo file="R5A_4246.jpg" alt="Microscope objectives above a glass slide on the microscope stage" card sizes="(min-width: 64em) 18rem, 100vw"}}

    Observe and assess

    Put your soil under the microscope. Identify which organisms are present, which are missing, and what that means for your plants, so every decision starts with what's actually alive in your soil.

  • {{photo file="R5A_3985.jpg" alt="A woman in a red hat turning a compost pile with a pitchfork, the white cover pulled back" card sizes="(min-width: 64em) 18rem, 100vw"}}

    Incubate the biology

    Grow the beneficial organisms your soil needs by making BioComplete™ compost, not just organic matter, but a living culture, built deliberately.

  • {{photo file="tile-extract-filter-bags.jpg" alt="Wooden stands holding white filter bags over a table in a barn, with black buckets on the floor below" card sizes="(min-width: 64em) 18rem, 100vw"}}

    Multiply and apply

    A small amount of biologically rich compost goes a long way. Compost extracts and teas multiply that life and carry it across whole fields and gardens: to the soil and to the plants themselves.

  • {{photo file="carrots-raised-garden-bed.jpg" alt="Young carrot plants growing in rows in a raised wooden garden bed, lit by low sun" card sizes="(min-width: 64em) 18rem, 100vw"}}

    Manage and monitor holistically

    Keep observing as the life rebuilds structure, cycles nutrients, and protects your plants, and adjust your practices so the benefits compound, season after season.
