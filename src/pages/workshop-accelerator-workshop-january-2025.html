Accelerator Workshop January 2025

Kampia, Cyprus · 13–24 January 2025

This workshop has ended.

In January 2025, we held our Accelerator Workshop at Riverland Bio Farm in Kampia, a village in the Machairas hills of Nicosia district, Cyprus. Riverland produces high-quality organic products with care for animal welfare, and it welcomes visitors for farm tours and environmental education.

Students arrived from 19 countries, which made Cyprus the most diverse group the school had gathered at the time. Like every Accelerator Workshop, it gave them ten days of hands-on training in making compost and biological liquid amendments, side by side with our mentors and with each other. One student wrote: "I loved every single session. I found them very well paced, especially practical approaches and mentors sharing their experiences and opinions."

One student shows how far a single workshop can reach. Dr. Hongyan Lu, from Chengdu, earned a PhD in organic waste recycling in Germany, and in 2021 she co-founded the Soil Life Research and Restoration Lab at Sichuan University, which works directly with farmers. After the Cyprus workshop, she became the school's first certified consultant in China.

Students come from all over the world and take what they learn back to their own corner of the globe. For Hongyan, that corner is Sichuan, where the Soil Food Web approach now has its first certified consultant.
