Accelerator Workshop January 2025

Kampia, Cyprus · 13–24 January 2025

This workshop has ended.

Nineteen countries, one compost yard

The January 2025 Accelerator Workshop took place at Riverland Bio Farm in Kampia, a village in the Machairas hills of Nicosia district (workshop page; Visit Nicosia). Riverland was created to produce high-quality organic products with care for animal welfare, and it welcomes visitors for farm tours and environmental education (Welcome Magazine).

Students came from 19 countries, which made the Cyprus group the school's most diverse so far (October 2025 newsletter). When the workshop was open, its page invited students to learn to make BioComplete™ compost, extracts and teas with Dr. Elaine Ingham (workshop page).

What the workshop was for

Like the other Accelerator Workshops of 2025, Cyprus gave students an in-person pathway through the hands-on stages of our advanced training, equivalent to the regular pathway (Enrollment Agreement, March 2025). In the standard format, the group builds several compost piles at once, some designed to succeed and some designed to fail, runs nematode extractions and protozoan infusions, makes compost extracts and teas, and checks everything under the microscope (Workshop Handbook, March 2025).

Students said the format did exactly what its name promises. One wrote: "The CTPAW is a unique opportunity to learn different ways of doing things in a short space of time, to confront and resolve many real situations not encountered in the online courses, and to forge strong community crossborder links within the Soil Food Web network (thus giving substance/life to the SFW). We learn from the realities of mentors (FANTASTIC TEAM!) and students, as well as from co-creation. The CTPAW is well named: it really is an accelerator" (January 2025 student, quoted on the UK Accelerator Workshop page). Another wrote: "I loved every single session. I found them very well paced, especially practical approaches and mentors sharing their experiences and opinions…" (January 2025 student, quoted on the UK Accelerator Workshop page).

What one workshop made possible

One student shows how far a single workshop can reach. Dr. Hongyan Lu, from Chengdu, earned a PhD in organic waste recycling in Germany and in 2021 co-founded the Soil Life Research and Restoration Lab at Sichuan University, which works directly with farmers (Find a Consultant). After attending the Cyprus workshop she became the school's first certified consultant in China (October 2025 newsletter).

Why it matters

Cyprus is running out of water. In early 2025, around the time of the workshop, the island's reservoirs stood at 26 percent of capacity, and by February 2026 they had fallen to 13.8 percent (Cyprus Mail). Desalination now covers about 70 percent of urban drinking water needs (FastForward). The wider region is warming 20 percent faster than the rest of the world (Union for the Mediterranean).

The land is under the same pressure. According to an EU Joint Research Centre report, 45 percent of Cyprus's land meets the criteria for desertification, and 61 percent of Cypriots live in affected areas, the highest share in the EU (Politis). Cypriot soils average only 1.5 percent organic carbon, and a study in the Troodos mountains found just 1.0 percent on productive farmland (Djuma et al., 2020). Each 1 percent increase in soil organic matter lets the top foot of soil hold about 3,400 more gallons of plant-available water per acre (University of Nebraska-Lincoln Water). On an island this dry, building soil biology is also a way of storing water.

The Cypriot government is moving in the same direction. Its Ministry of Agriculture coordinates LIFE AgrOassis, an EU-funded project that aims to raise soil organic matter by 30 percent and build two compost facilities on the island (European Commission). Training people who know how to make biologically complete compost gives projects like this the skills they need. With students from 19 countries, the workshop also sent those skills to dry and degraded land around the world.
