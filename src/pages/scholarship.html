Scholarships

Our scholarship program extends the Foundation's training to farmers, land stewards, and community leaders who couldn't otherwise afford it, with priority for applicants working in under-resourced regions where restored soil changes lives fastest.

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Tell us about you

    A short application: where you are, what you steward, and what you would do with the training.

  2. Step 2

    We review in cohorts

    Applications are read in batches rather than one at a time, so everyone in a round is compared fairly.

    Review cadence and criteria: how often a round is read and what is weighed. The education team confirms; Evan.

  3. Step 3

    Start learning

    Scholars join the same courses and the same community as every other student, with the same refund rule: a full refund within 30 days of registration, provided you have not accessed a substantial portion of the course content (Terms, section 3).
