Accelerator Workshop May 2027

New Mexico, United States · 3–14 May 2027

Photo by Raychel Sanner on Unsplash

Overview

Join the Soil Food Web Foundation team in New Mexico for a ten-day, hands-on Accelerator Workshop and gain a year's worth of experience in two weeks (UK Accelerator Workshop page). Students build BioComplete™ compost piles from scratch, make extracts and teas, and work at the microscope in small groups with a mentor (UK Accelerator Workshop page).
