Accelerator Workshop May 2027

New Mexico, United States · 3–14 May 2027

Overview

Join the Soil Food Web School team in New Mexico for a ten-day, hands-on Accelerator Workshop. Students build BioComplete™ compost piles from scratch, make extracts and teas, and work at the microscope in small groups with a mentor.
