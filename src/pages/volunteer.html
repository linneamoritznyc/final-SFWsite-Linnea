Pick the way you want to help

Every role here comes from a real need at the Foundation. Choose one, or tell us about something we haven't thought of.

  • Host a compost day

    Open your farm, garden, or school plot for a hands-on build. We send the guide and help you promote it.

    Time: One weekend, a few times a year

  • Share your microscope

    Trained in the Foundation courses? Help local growers read their soil at meetups or over video.

    Time: 2 to 4 hours a month

  • Translate the science

    One course now has Spanish subtitles. Help us bring the rest into Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, Telugu, Swahili, and more, or review translations for accuracy.

    Time: Flexible, remote

  • Run a local meetup

    Gather growers near you to share compost results and questions. We send a starter guide and a direct line to staff.

    Time: About 5 hours a month

  • Document the work

    Photograph and film workshops and field results. Your footage feeds our courses and social channels.

    Time: Per event

  • Lend professional skills

    Design, web, grant writing, data, legal, accounting. Small nonprofits run on generous experts.

    Time: Project based

  • Collect field data

    Record soil samples, plant health, and yields on trial sites so results can be published openly.

    Time: Seasonal, a few hours a visit

  • Welcome new students

    Alumni can answer first questions, join study calls, and help newcomers find their footing in the courses.

    Time: 1 to 2 hours a week

  • Staff an event table

    Represent the Foundation at farmers markets, garden shows, and conferences near you. We ship the materials.

    Time: Per event

  • Write and research

    Draft blog posts, fact-check claims against their sources, and summarise new soil biology papers.

    Time: Flexible, remote

  • Host a fundraiser

    Run a dinner, open garden day, or online campaign that funds scholarships for growers who can't pay.

    Time: One-off

  • Share your own idea

    Have an idea or a skill we haven't listed? Tell us in the form and we'll find a way to use it.

    Time: Up to you

  • Put your farm on the map

    Add your farm, garden or project to the Community Map so growers near you can find you.

    Time: 15 minutes

  • Run a jar test

    Shake soil samples in jars with growers near you and compare the layers. It starts good conversations about soil structure.

    Time: One afternoon

  • Interview a grower

    Record a 20-minute conversation with a farmer who uses biology. We turn it into a story for the website and social media.

    Time: A few hours per story

  • Lend land or a space

    Offer a field for a demo pile, a barn for a workshop or a room for a meetup.

    Time: When you can

  • Move supplies

    Drive microscopes, compost or event kits to where they are needed in your region.

    Time: Now and then

  • Start a worm bin

    Keep a worm bin at your school, office or apartment block and show others how it works.

    Time: 10 minutes a week

  • Spread the word

    Share webinars, courses and scholarships in the grower groups, newsletters and forums you are already in.

    Time: 10 minutes a week

  • Host a watch party

    Invite friends or your garden club to watch a webinar together and talk it through afterwards.

    Time: One evening

  • Answer questions online

    Answer beginner questions in community forums and comment threads, and point people to the right course or resource.

    Time: An hour a week

Grow at your own pace

  1. 1 · Surface

    First task

    Share a webinar, film one workshop, translate one page. You see what the work is like, with no commitment.

  2. 2 · Topsoil

    Regular role

    Pick a role from the list above and give a few hours a month. You join the volunteer channel and hear about openings first.

  3. 3 · Subsoil

    Lead a project

    Run a translation, organise a meetup series, or coordinate an event. You work directly with Foundation staff.

  4. 4 · Deep roots

    Train new volunteers

    Welcome and guide the people who join after you, and help shape how the volunteer programme grows.

Questions people ask first

Do I need a science background?

No. Many roles need hands, a camera, a language, or a professional skill. Microscope roles ask for some training, and the Foundation Courses cover it.

Do I have to be a student at the School?

No. Students and alumni are welcome, and so is anyone who cares about living soil.

Can I volunteer from home?

Yes. Translation, design, writing, grant work, and data roles are fully remote.

I have land. Can I host something?

Yes. Farms, market gardens, school plots, and community gardens all make good sites for a compost day. Tell us about your land in the form.

Is volunteering paid?

Volunteer roles are unpaid. Regular volunteers get a starter guide for their role and direct contact with staff.

How it works

  1. You send the form. Takes about two minutes.
  2. We reply within a week. A volunteer coordinator reads your skills and region.
  3. We match you with a remote project, a local meetup, or an upcoming event.
  4. You start with a short welcome call and a starter guide for your role.
