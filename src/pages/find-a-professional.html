Find a professional

Work with a Soil Food Web School Graduate

Search our directory to find someone in our community who can support you on your regenerative journey. You may be looking to analyze the quality of a compost product you are considering, wanting to diagnose the health of your soil, or working on a full scale transition for your farm.

Soil Food Web Lab Techs have completed our Soil Microscopy Course, and are equipped to analyze soil and soil amendments like compost and compost tea. Soil Food Web Consultants have completed all four modules of our Complete Practicum: Soil Microscopy, BioComplete™ Compost Production, Biological Liquid Amendments and a culminating Field Trial. Our Consultants are equipped to use this toolkit to support others in implementing biological approaches to land care and food production.
