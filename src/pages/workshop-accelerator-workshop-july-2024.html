Accelerator Workshop July 2024

Área Metropolitana de San José, Costa Rica · 15–26 July 2024

This workshop has ended.

Overview

The July 2024 Accelerator Workshop took place at Armonía Agricultura Orgánica in Heredia, part of the San José metro area of Costa Rica (Workshop Handbook March 2025; workshop page). Armonía is a campus with primary and secondary schools and an organic farm that feeds visitors at its on-site restaurant (October 2025 newsletter). The farm has terraced slopes and a greenhouse (photo), and it hosted the school twice more in 2025 (Workshop Handbook March 2025; October 2025 newsletter).

The school invited students from its Consultant Training Program and Grower Training Program to "participate in a hands-on composting workshop to advance their skills in creating biologically complete compost" (Beneath the Surface newsletter). Photos from 16 July show students working through feedstocks, wood chips and buckets, digging into the soil with their bare hands, and teams posing with their finished piles in wire mesh bins (photo).

On 18 July the whole group gathered for a photo on the terraced slope in front of the farm's greenhouse (photo).

Why it matters

This workshop brought consultants and growers together in one place: the people who will advise farms and the people who run them (Beneath the Surface newsletter). It took place in July, in the middle of the Central Valley's rainy season, which runs from May to October, so students built and monitored their piles in the wettest months of the year (Workshop Handbook March 2025). Armonía went on to host two more workshops in 2025 (October 2025 newsletter).
