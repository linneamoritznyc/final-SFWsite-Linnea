Boilerplate

The Soil Food Web Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization founded in 2025 to carry forward the work of Dr. Elaine Ingham, the pioneering scientist who mapped the soil food web: the living community of microorganisms that drives soil fertility, plant health, and ecosystem function. The Foundation trains students in 100+ countries through the Soil Food Web School, is opening its research and trial data for public study, and supports a global community of practitioners restoring soil on farms and ecosystems worldwide. Learn more at soilfoodweb.com. (501(c)(3), EIN 39-4439236.)
