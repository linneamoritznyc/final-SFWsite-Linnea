Ecosystem Restoration Workshop February 2024

Yucatán, México · 4–10 February 2024

This workshop has ended.

In February 2024, we ran a six-day Ecosystem Restoration Workshop at the Earth Connection Center Biological Reserve on Mexico's Yucatán Peninsula, together with Ecosystem Restoration Communities. The reserve covers 39 hectares of dry tropical forest, with a cenote and Mayan ruins on the land.

For many years the forest was clear cut and used as a cattle ranch. Canadian ecologist Paul Morris and Mexican agronomist Sophia Ortiz founded the reserve in 2015 to bring it back, and since restoration began in 2016, the land has responded. The soil has turned brown, an organic layer has formed, the canopy has started to close and wildlife has returned. For students, the reserve itself was the textbook.

The workshop was built for students of our Introduction to Ecosystem Restoration course, created with Ecosystem Restoration Communities, so they could put the online course into practice. Paul and Sophia led them through a restoration project step by step. Students assessed a healthy reference forest, collected soil samples, practised identifying species, learned nursery skills and built habitat structures. They also worked through the harder questions of restoration: removing the pressures that degrade land, raising money and involving the local community. Ashleigh Brown of Ecosystem Restoration Communities supported the week and documented it, so restoration communities around the world could run workshops like it.

By the end, students had written a restoration plan for a real plot on the reserve, ready to take what they learned out into the world.
