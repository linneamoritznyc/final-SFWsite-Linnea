Tools

sMApp, the microscope tool

Count what you see through the microscope, type the numbers in, and sMApp returns bacterial and fungal biomass, the fungal to bacterial ratio, and protozoa and nematode numbers. It builds the same report a Soil Food Web laboratory sends back on a posted sample.

Work is organised as portfolios, clients and projects, so one practitioner can follow many sites over the years. It needs a microscope that reaches about 400x. A phone camera will not do it. None of the data you enter is kept by Soil Food Web.

Put it on your phone

Use it in a browser at www.soilmapp.com or add it to your home screen for quick access.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open www.soilmapp.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

Android

  1. Open www.soilmapp.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu button, the three dots.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen. Some phones say Install app.
