How we're funded

Where our money comes from, and where it goes

The Soil Food Web Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. The Soil Food Web School is a program of the Soil Food Web Foundation.

Where it comes from

Course fees

The Soil Food Web School, our education program, was a for-profit company until October 2025. It is now operated by the Foundation. Course fees are program revenue; after the cost of delivering the courses they fund scholarships, open research and field projects.

Payments for courses, workshops and programs are fees for services and are not tax-deductible charitable gifts.

Donations

Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Online donations are processed by our payment partner, Givebutter, and a receipt is sent by email for every gift.

Income by source for the first year (course fees, donations, grants, other), as amounts or percentages, with the period they cover. Evan supplies them from the Foundation's books.

Where it goes

After the cost of delivering the courses, course fees and gifts fund three things:

  • Scholarships for students who could not otherwise afford the training.
  • Open research that anyone can use.
  • Field projects that turn knowledge into restored land.

Spending split in percent (course delivery, scholarships, research, field projects, operations) for the same period. Evan; Decision 9 in this repo's copy deck.

Scholarships

Our scholarship program extends the Foundation's training to farmers, land stewards, and community leaders who couldn't otherwise afford it, with priority for applicants working in under-resourced regions.

Scholarship-designated gifts go directly to student access.
