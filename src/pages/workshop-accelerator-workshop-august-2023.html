Accelerator Workshop August 2023

Área Metropolitana de San José, Costa Rica · 7–18 August 2023

This workshop has ended.

In August 2023, we brought our ten-day Accelerator Workshop to the San José area of Costa Rica for the first time. It was the start of a long relationship with the country. We have returned three times since, and another workshop is planned for January 2027.

The workshop brought together students from our consultant and grower training programs to learn how to make biological amendments by hand, working alongside the Soil Food Web team for ten days.

Biological amendments such as compost return organic matter and active microbial life to the soil, and the evidence for what that does is strong. In a 2025 study of 56 farmer-managed fields, fields treated with compost scored 31 percent higher on soil health than comparison fields. Their soil life was 45 percent more active, and their soils could hold on to 42 percent more nutrients, while wheat yields held steady.

Students come from all over the world and take what they learn back to their own corner of the globe. In 2023, that work began in Costa Rica, and it has grown with every visit since.
