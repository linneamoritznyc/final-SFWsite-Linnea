Why monthly giving matters

Single donations are always appreciated. But by becoming a sustaining donors, you help us plan our impact through the stability provided by consistent, reliable support.

We are committed to data-backed programs and boots-on-the-ground action where it is most impactful. We work to maintain efficiency throughout the entire process, and are committed to providing programming where it is needed, not only where it can be afforded.

Every dollar donated on a recurring basis helps us become even more efficient by letting us plan ahead. Your sustaining support is vital to our mission to reach real people willing to restore degraded land with vital soil health knowledge and skills.

Prefer to give another way — stock, DAF, bequest, or a corporate partnership? Contact us →
