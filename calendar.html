  • 19 to 30 October 2026

    Accelerator Workshop India 2026

    In collaboration with Isha Outreach. 19 to 30 October 2026 at Save Soil Farm, near the Isha Yoga Center, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Our first workshop on the subcontinent. Hands in soil, microscopes on tables, and a community milestone for the region.

    Enroll on the school site See the webinar series · Register interest in a future workshop

  • 1 to 7 December 2026

    Soil Health Week Pakistan 2026

    The second year of Soil Health Week in Pakistan. Co-led by Wild Soils UK (Nick Padwick) and TrashIt, a woman- and youth-led compost enterprise in Pakistan. The Soil Food Web Foundation is a supporting partner. Last year's event reached more than 600 participants across 60 districts.

    Read about last year's Soil Health Week · TrashIt

    Workshop
  • 25 August to 13 September 2026

    Designing the Regeneration webinar series

    This series celebrates regenerative design solutions to today's biggest challenges. Hosted by permaculture teachers Kavi Reddy and Delvin Solkinson, it features instructors from the upcoming Soil Food Web Permaculture Design Certificate, including Geoff Lawton and Mary Reynolds. Offered in partnership with the Permaculture Association, honoring the legacy of Graham Bell and Dr. Elaine Ingham.

    Register free

    Public webinar
  • 13 September 2026, 9:00 a.m. Pacific

    Rewilding and Restoring Native Ecosystems with Mary Reynolds

    Learn how to grow an Ark and restore native ecosystems. Mary Reynolds on becoming a guardian of the land and rewilding with acts of restorative kindness. Live presentation followed by audience questions.

    Register free

  • 13 September 2026, 11:00 a.m. Pacific

    Permaculture Design Certificate Info Session

    Meet the live session instructors and learn about Graham Bell's legacy Permaculture Design Certificate Course. Understand the topics, course formats, the feature map and design project. Meet other people around the world who are interested in joining this global classroom.
