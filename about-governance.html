How we’re governed, and where the money goes

Soil Food Web Foundation is a nonprofit public benefit corporation recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) public charity.

Legal status

Soil Food Web Foundation is a nonprofit public benefit corporation recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) public charity. EIN 39-4439236. Registered office: 5441 S Macadam Ave Ste N, Portland, Oregon 97239. Incorporated October 2025.

IRS determination letter, linked below

The School is a program of the Foundation

The Soil Food Web School, our education program, was a for-profit company until October 2025. It is now operated by the Foundation. Course fees are program revenue; after the cost of delivering the courses they fund scholarships, open research and field projects. In 2026 philanthropic gifts were roughly a quarter of revenue.

Payments for courses, workshops and programs are fees for services and are not tax-deductible charitable gifts.

Foundation records. DECISION 9 to confirm the figure for publication.
