Certified SFW Lab-Tech

Norman Higgins

New Zealand

I have spend the last 5 years or so soaking up every bit of knowledge I could find on healthy soil life. It is only since I started the SFW Foundation Courses that I feel I am no longer living off scraps I could source on the internet and I can finally get to the meat!

I am so excited to continue this amazing journey as I unlearn 30 years of agrochemical farming and move down the regenerative path to biological understanding.
