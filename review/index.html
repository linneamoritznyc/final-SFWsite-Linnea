  • Emails (bug 21). Staff addresses on staging use @soilfoodwebfoundation.org (ebuckman, jlnoel, loida, lwlewis, eric). They now read @soilfoodweb.com with the same name part. Stephanie McDaniel confirms each mailbox exists. Directory members keep their own business addresses.

  • Countries (bug 15). 100+ everywhere; staging's "more than 130 countries" lines now read 100+.
  • School wording (bug 17). "The Soil Food Web School is a program of the Soil Food Web Foundation" in the footer, About, Privacy and Terms; the doing-business-as sentence in Privacy and Terms is replaced. Legal review should confirm.
  • Funding sentence. From docs/copy-deck-v2.md section 5, on Home, About, Programs, Donate, Scholarship and in the footer: "Course fees are program revenue; after the cost of delivering the courses they fund scholarships, open research and field projects." It is the Ad Grant checklist's funding sentence until a final one is approved.
  • Light Green. #59A66C is 3:1 on white, so it is used for big numbers, rules and decoration only. Eyebrows and links use Food Web Green #156826 for contrast; staging uses the light green for eyebrows.
  • Maps. The staging directory and community maps are not rebuilt in this static preview (they need a map library and tile server); placeholders say so.
  • Images. Every image is checked against this repo first, by file name and by picture, and the repo copy is used when it exists; only images the repo does not have are downloaded from staging. All are WebP, at most 1600 px wide and under 300 KB. Nine images that staging lost in its migration are restored from the same posts on soilfoodweb.com.
