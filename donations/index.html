Donate

The Soil Food Web Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Every gift amplifies our impact by funding scholarships, supporting research initiatives, and increasing our capacity to take direct action on behalf of the living world.

Make a gift

The Givebutter donation form (monthly or one-time, preset amounts) embeds here on the live site. It cannot run in this static preview.

Prefer a plain link? Donate on Givebutter (givebutter.com/sfwf-donate).

Impact lines for the preset amounts, only once program costs are confirmed (for example what a monthly gift funds). Do not invent amounts. Operations confirms; Evan.

Before launch: make a $1 test donation and check the thank-you page and the emailed receipt with the EIN; set the Givebutter tip to 0% or off; name the campaign goal and what the money raised goes to. Stephanie McDaniel.

For your records

Soil Food Web Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. EIN 39-4439236. Oregon DOJ registration #71264.

Online donations are processed by our payment partner, Givebutter, and a receipt is sent by email for every gift.

Where a gift comes with something in return, such as a ticket, a place at an event, or merchandise, only the amount above the fair market value of that benefit is deductible. We state the deductible amount on the receipt for any such gift.

Read our privacy policy and the donations section of our Terms.

Monthly giving helps us plan ahead

Prefer to give another way or have questions about our work? Contact us.

Course fees and gifts are different

Payments for courses, workshops and programs are fees for services and are not tax-deductible charitable gifts. The Soil Food Web School is a program of the Soil Food Web Foundation, and course revenue funds the Foundation's work, but a course purchase is a purchase. Donations made on this page are gifts and are deductible to the extent allowed by law.
