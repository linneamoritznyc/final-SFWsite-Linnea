2. FIRST FLAP, WHY BIOLOGY  [outside, left: seen when the cover opens]

WHY BIOLOGY

Feed the organisms that feed your plants.

[Photograph: Elaine Flower Shirt Microscope.png]

Dr. Elaine Ingham at the microscope.

Plants don't feed alone.

Bacteria and fungi produce enzymes that break down minerals and organic matter and free the nutrients they contain. When protozoa and nematodes consume those bacteria and fungi, the surplus is released right at the root, in plant-available form.

Bacteria produce glues that bind soil particles into microaggregates; fungal hyphae weave those into larger macroaggregates. The result behaves like a sponge, holding water, letting air flow in and resisting erosion.

These microbes are often lost in land that has been chemically managed or compacted. Biologically complete compost is designed to restore them.

OUR STORY
Dr. Elaine Ingham mapped the Soil Food Web and taught the world to restore it. The Soil Food Web Foundation, established in October 2025, carries her work forward with growers on working land.

4. INSIDE MIDDLE, ON YOUR FARM

ON YOUR FARM

We start with
your soil.

[Photograph: Test tubes with sample_.jpg]

Soil samples beside the microscope.

The steps are the same at every scale: look at the biology under a microscope, add what is missing to rebalance the microbial content, then measure again to confirm microbial counts are going in the expected directions.

What we test before we recommend

Diagnostic first. Every recommendation starts with a biological analysis under a microscope.

Measurable. We provide a relative count of various microbes present in your soil or compost sample.

Adaptation and retrofitting. We help you convert your current operations to the Soil Food Web Approach, at any scale.

5. INSIDE RIGHT, LEARN

LEARN

Learn to grow your own soil biology.

[Photograph: Loida Teaching 3.JPG]

A mentor teaching on working land.

Everyone starts with the Foundation Courses. Where you go next depends on whether you want to apply your skills to your own land or help other farmers improve their soils.

Start learning

The Foundation Courses give you the biological literacy to understand what is actually happening in your soil, why it matters, and what you can do about it. They are online, self-paced, take about 150 hours to complete, and are a prerequisite to any Advanced Program.

Go professional

Learn the practical application of the theory you learned in the Foundation Courses through mentor-led Advanced Programs on Soil Microscopy, BioComplete™ Compost Production, and Biological Liquid Amendments and Field Trial.

Learn in person

Accelerator Workshops are immersive, two-week, hands-on intensives where you produce BioComplete™ compost and biological liquid amendments live, hosted with a partner on their land. Gain a year's worth of hands-on experience in one immersive workshop.

Where it leads

Careers in soil biology analysis as SFW Lab-Technicians, and working with farmers and land stewards to implement the Soil Food Web Approach as SFW Consultants.
