## Panel widths are not equal

The tucked panel is 1/4 in narrower so it does not bind. The other two are
equal, so the cover meets the folded edge.

| Page | Panel widths, left to right | Folds from left trim |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1, outside | **3.5**, 3.75, 3.75 | 3.5 in, 7.25 in |
| 2, inside | 3.75, 3.75, **3.5** | 3.75 in, 7.5 in |

## Three notes

1. **The two pages are mirrored on purpose. Please impose as supplied.** The
   tuck panel falls on the left of one side of the sheet and the right of the
   other. Normalizing both pages to the same panel order puts every panel 1/4
   in out of register with its own back.

2. **Total ink coverage is 309% max.** Fine for coated sheetfed. If you are
   running uncoated or web, tell us your limit and we will re-separate.

3. **Please scan the five QR codes on the press proof**, gold one first — it is
   the lightest ink and has the least margin. If any fails we have darker
   versions ready.

The file's output intent is a generic CMYK profile. Convert to your own press
condition.
