Design system, Stage 1

Tokens, the type scale, colour, spacing, the one card, the icon set, and the three global pieces: header, overlay menu, footer. Everything on this page is real code from site.css and site.js. Open the Menu button to see the overlay.

Demo built 7 September 2026. Items awaiting real data are marked as placeholders and look like this:

the cut-out moss form in the overlay corner is a grey polygon until a real moss photograph from the SFW library is cut and supplied by Stephanie.

Colour

Paper is bone, a shade cooler than the brand’s Organic Cream, so the ground does not land in the cream-and-serif default. Brand green stays for every link and button because it passes AA on paper. Gold appears on Donate and nowhere else. The overlay and dark bands use the brand’s deep green.

Contrast on paper #ECE8DC: ink 14.6:1, Soil Brown 9.6:1, Food Web Green 6.5:1, Soil Brown on gold 4.9:1, paper on moss 11.4:1. All pass WCAG 2.1 AA for body text. The lighter legacy green #59A66C and Legacy Purple are not in the system; they can be added as tokens if a page needs them.

Typography

Two families, both open licence and self-hosted at 204 kB total: EB Garamond for headings, an old-style serif in the spirit of the journals Elaine published in; Source Sans 3 for body, kept from the brand guide. Scale is 1.25 from an 18px body. Flush left, ragged right, measure 62 characters.

Rules the type follows: no tracked-out all-caps eyebrows, no numbered sections unless the content is a sequence, no arrows on links, no accent word in a headline.

Layout: strata

The page is a column of horizontal bands separated by hairline rules, like a soil profile. Inside each band a 12-column grid, used asymmetrically: heading in the left five columns, running text from column seven. Nothing is centred. Depth is made by a second stratum of paper, never by shadows.

Left column

Four columns. Headings, captions, the short thing.

Right column

Eight columns. Running text, tables, the long thing. On screens under 768px both stack, in this order.

Living behaviour

One hairline root per long page, drawn as you scroll and undrawn as you scroll back. It is a single SVG path with stroke-dashoffset set from scroll position in twelve lines of script. Under reduced motion the line is simply there, complete. Scroll this band up and down to see it grow along the left edge.

The second living element is the cut-out: a hand-cut form of real moss or bark placed at one corner of a band, sparse at the top of a page and accumulating toward the bottom, never more than two in view. It is placed, not animated. The overlay menu carries one in its lower right corner, currently the placeholder polygon.

Header, overlay, footer

The header above and the footer below are the real templates. The header keeps four things: the wordmark with the nonprofit line beneath it, What’s happening, Student login, and Donate, which stays visible at every width. The Menu button floods the viewport deep green.

Overlay behaviour

  • Opens from Menu, closes on Close, Escape, or any link.
  • Focus moves to Close on open and returns to Menu on close; Tab is trapped inside.
  • Body scroll locks while open.
  • Six sections as an accordion; opening one closes the others. Now is in the accordion here so the demo shows all six, but on the live site it is reached from the header as the brief specifies.
  • Right column, Happening now: three dated items, then the link to Now.

Wordmark

Set in type, not a logo file. The brand guide’s logo artwork was not in the redesign folder; when it is supplied, it replaces the text wordmark at a minimum of 140px wide, on paper only.
