Ecosystem Restoration Workshop February 2024

Yucatán, México · 4–10 February 2024

This workshop has ended.

Overview

In February 2024, the school ran an in-person workshop in Mexico with Ecosystem Restoration Camps (Beneath the Surface newsletter). The six days took place at the Earth Connection Center Biological Reserve, 39 hectares of dry tropical forest on the Yucatán Peninsula with a cenote and Mayan ruins, where the land is being restored through native planting and agroecology (workshop page; Earth Connection Center). It was open only to current and past students of the Introduction to Ecosystem Restoration course (workshop page). Participants assessed a reference ecosystem, took soil samples, identified species, learned tree and plant nursery skills, built habitat structures, and wrote a restoration plan for a real site (workshop page). The leaders were Paul Morris, a Certified Ecological Restoration Practitioner, and agronomy engineer Sophia Ortiz, with Ashleigh Brown of Ecosystem Restoration Communities documenting the week (workshop page).
