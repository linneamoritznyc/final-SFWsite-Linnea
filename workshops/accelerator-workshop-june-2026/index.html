Accelerator Workshop June 2026

Norfolk, United Kingdom · 15–26 June 2026

This workshop has ended.

In June 2026, we returned to Wild Ken Hill for our second Accelerator Workshop on the Norfolk coast, and all 25 places sold out. The estate runs from the sea through coastal scrub, freshwater marshes, heathland and wood pasture to farmland, and more than 2,500 species have been recorded there. Its farmland already runs on Soil Food Web based composting, so students learned on land where the method is at work.

This year the group built and monitored eight compost piles. Students worked in teams of four or five, each with its own instructor who reviewed their recipes and followed every pile with them. With eight piles side by side, everyone could watch how different feedstocks, recipes and management choices played out in real time. When a pile started to fail, students diagnosed it on the spot and learned how to turn it around.

Building and turning the piles by hand took real teamwork, and it built leadership along the way. Students also made compost extracts with several methods, brewed compost tea at small and large scale, and practised applying liquid amendments in the field. They plotted a sampling grid for large-scale field work, ran nematode extractions from the materials they had helped produce, and studied it all under the microscope with an expert beside them. The training team brought farming, biology, agronomy and permaculture to the same compost yard.

Students come from all over the world and take what they learn back to their own corner of the globe. From Wild Ken Hill, they took home the experience of eight piles in ten days.
