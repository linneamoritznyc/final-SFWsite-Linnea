Accelerator Workshop January 2025

Kampia, Cyprus · 13–24 January 2025

This workshop has ended.

The group under burlap bunting

Overview

The January 2025 Accelerator Workshop took place in Kampia, Cyprus (workshop page), and opened a year in which the school ran four in-person workshops across three countries (October 2025 newsletter).

Students came from 19 countries, which made the Cyprus group the school's most diverse so far (October 2025 newsletter). Among them was Advanced Programs student Hongyan Lu, who has since become the first SFW certified consultant in China (October 2025 newsletter). When the workshop was open, its page invited students to meet Dr. Elaine Ingham in Cyprus and learn to make BioComplete™ compost, extracts and teas with her (workshop page).

Students worked at compound microscopes and kept hand-drawn notebooks of the organisms they found in their samples. In the group photo, taken under burlap bunting, many of them wear the school's "I'm part of the soil-ution" shirts (photo).

Why it matters

Cyprus is on the front line of desertification in Europe. A 2018 report by the European Court of Auditors cites studies finding that 99 percent of the island is vulnerable to desertification, and Cyprus is one of 13 EU countries that have declared themselves affected (European Court of Auditors). Kampia sits on a plateau at about 500 metres in the Machairas hills (Visit Nicosia). It lies in the Cyprus Mediterranean forests ecoregion, where rainfall is falling, water is overused for farming, and centuries of overgrazing and fire have turned woodland into shrubland (One Earth). The village's former copper and pyrite mine, now the red Kokkinonero lake, sits just outside it (Visit Nicosia). Training students from 19 countries in a place where soil loss is already visible gave them a case study for dry, degraded land worldwide.
