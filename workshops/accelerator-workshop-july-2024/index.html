Accelerator Workshop July 2024

Área Metropolitana de San José, Costa Rica · 15–26 July 2024

This workshop has ended.

In July 2024, we held our first Accelerator Workshop at Armonía Agricultura Orgánica, an organic farm and school campus in La Asunción de Belén, Heredia. Armonía's mission is to be an example of organic farming done responsibly, with locally grown food at its core. Students worked in covered and open-air spaces on the farm, with indoor classrooms for microscopy.

July falls in Heredia's rainy season, which runs from May to October, so students learned to manage their compost piles in wet conditions.

The workshop brought together students from our consultant and grower training programs, the people who will advise farms and the people who run them, for hands-on training in creating biologically complete compost. That first visit also started a partnership: Armonía welcomed us back twice in 2025, in March and October.

Students come from all over the world and take what they learn back to their own corner of the globe. Some left ready for the next step. As one student put it: "Excellent all round. Great exchange of experiences and ideas. Learned lots. Am now ready to take consultancy course."
