Accelerator Workshop March 2025

Área Metropolitana de San José, Costa Rica · 3–14 March 2025

This workshop has ended.

In March 2025, we returned to Armonía Agricultura Orgánica in La Asunción de Belén, Heredia, 11 km north of San José. It was our second workshop there, eight months after the first, and this time we came in the dry season.

The heart of this workshop was the mentoring. Students worked in small teams, each with a dedicated mentor, and the group built five compost piles and ran them all at once, some designed to succeed and some to fail. From day three, they checked every pile each morning and afternoon and turned it when needed. One student wrote that it was "great to hear the contrasting applications from all the mentors and environments they are in," and that it was "good to have your crew in a small team with dedicated mentor."

Mentor Gerald Ramírez showed students how to make liquid amendments. In the second week, the group moved on to compost teas, nematode extraction, protozoan infusions and humic acid extraction. Students and mentors worked side by side at the microscopes, checking compost and amendments at each stage, and the group came up with some great techniques of their own for mixing compost materials.

Donell Frank's advice to future students captures the ten days well: "Be prepared to work hard, listen, and watch everything very intently! Taking good notes helps tremendously. You will meet some awesome people and make new friends for life."

Students come from all over the world and take what they learn back to their own corner of the globe, along with a few of those new friends.
