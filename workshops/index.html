In-person workshops

Two weeks on a real site with instructors and a small group: compost piles built, samples read under the microscope, a year's worth of solo trial and error compressed. Workshops have run in Costa Rica, England and New Mexico; the first in Asia is this October.

Accelerator Workshop India 2026

19 to 30 October 2026 · Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu

In collaboration with Isha Outreach. 19 to 30 October 2026 at Save Soil Farm, near the Isha Yoga Center, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Our first workshop on the subcontinent. Hands in soil, microscopes on tables, and a community milestone for the region.
