Accelerator Workshop February 2026

Santa Fe, United States · 23 Feb – 6 Mar 2026

This workshop has ended.

In February 2026, we held our Accelerator Workshop at Synergia Ranch, about 15 miles south of Santa Fe, New Mexico. It was our dryland workshop, high in the desert at about 6,300 feet, where water is scarce and every bit of soil organic matter counts.

Synergia has a remarkable story. John P. Allen founded the ranch in 1969, and in 1984 he launched the Biosphere 2 project with Ed Bass. When the orchard was planted, a compost pit three feet wide and three feet deep was dug under every tree, and since 2019 the farm has used a no-till approach. Decades of that care show in the soil. Recent tests found 4.5 to 5.5 percent organic matter in the ranch's soils, three to four times the 1.3 percent measured in soils nearby.

That made Synergia a living example of what students came to learn. The Accelerator Workshop packs the hands-on part of our advanced training into ten days: building BioComplete™ compost piles from scratch in small teams with a mentor, monitoring and turning them, making compost extracts and teas, and checking the results under the microscope. Together, that work covers the learning outcomes of our BioComplete™ Compost Production and Biological Liquid Amendments courses.

Students come from all over the world and take what they learn back to their own corner of the globe. At Synergia, they learned it on a farm that shows how far patient soil care can go, even in the desert.
