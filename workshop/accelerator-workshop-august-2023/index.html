Accelerator Workshop August 2023

San José metro, Costa Rica · 7 to 18 August 2023

CONFIRM with Stephanie: the CTPFW photos may be from October 2025

Dates
7 to 18 August 2023
Status
Closed
Instructors
CONFIRM

The August 2023 workshop took place in the San José metro area of Costa Rica. (workshop page) The school ran it as a CTP Fast Track Workshop, the earlier name for its ten-day intensive.

CONFIRM: source for the CTP Fast Track name; it comes from the enrollment page URL found in an earlier search.

CONFIRM: venue, and whether it was Armonía Agricultura Orgánica.

The workshop was built for students in the Consultant Training Program and the Grower Training Program. (workshop page) For ten days they worked alongside the Soil Food Web team, making biological amendments by hand. (workshop page)

CONFIRM: what students built and practiced, and who taught.

Graduates received their certificates under a white tent with tropical plants behind them.

CONFIRM: number of students and countries, and a student quote with permission.

Why it matters

This is the earliest Costa Rica workshop on the site, and the start of a run of five workshops in the San José area in four years: August 2023, July 2024, March 2025, October 2025 and January 2027. (SFW workshops page) Coming back to the same region again and again lets the school build a local network of consultants and growers.
