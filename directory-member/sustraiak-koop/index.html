Sustraiak Habitat Design Koop

About

Sustraiak Habitat Design, a Cooperative dedicated to promoting the agroecological transition using Regenerative Agriculture tools and from a Social and Solidarity Economy perspective. Since 2016, we have developed over 120 projects, worked with more than 300 farmers, delivered over 200 training sessions, and facilitated the regeneration of more than 4,000 hectares.
