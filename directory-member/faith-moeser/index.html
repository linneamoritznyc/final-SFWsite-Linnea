Faith Moeser

Soil Dynamix

About

Faith Moeser runs Soil Dynamix out of Spokane, Washington. After starting a garlic farm, Faith faced the same challenges many farmers encounter—declining soil health and inconsistent yields. That journey led her to the Soil Food Web School, where she saw firsthand the transformative power of soil biology. Through Soil Dynamix, Faith combines biological principles with mineral balancing to help farmers and gardeners build healthy soils and grow productive, resilient crops.
