Scholarships

Increasing the accessibility of our programs and the diversity of our community

Our scholarship program extends Soil Food Web education to emerging scientists, farmers, land stewards, and community leaders who couldn’t otherwise afford it — with priority for applicants working in regions where soil fertility has the greatest capacity to impact lives.
