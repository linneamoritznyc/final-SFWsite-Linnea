The year, month by month

  1. Oct 2025

    Dr. Ingham retires; the Foundation launches

    Dr. Elaine Ingham retires from teaching, and the Soil Food Web Foundation launches as a nonprofit to carry her work forward. Retirement announcement · Launch announcement

  2. Oct 2025

    Soil Health Week in Pakistan

    Wild Soils UK and TrashIt bring the Soil Food Web Approach to Pakistan. Read the story

  3. Nov 2025

    A free webinar series

    "A Living Legacy: The Science of the Soil Food Web" opens to everyone. About the series

  4. Feb 2026

    Dr. Elaine Ingham passes away

    Dr. Ingham passed away on February 16, 2026. Obituary

  5. Feb 2026

    A new board member; Advanced Programs reopen

    Eric Feiler joins the board, and the Soil Food Web School's Advanced Programs reopen. Welcoming Eric · Advanced Programs

  6. Apr 2026

    The first Permaculture Design Certificate

    The Soil Food Web School launches its first Permaculture Design Certificate course. Read more

  7. Oct 2026

    One year on

    The Foundation celebrates its first year with the community. The anniversary event
